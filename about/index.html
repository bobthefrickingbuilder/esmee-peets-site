Esmée Peets

Artist & arts educator, Ottawa / Montréal. Painting, graphite drawing, and collaborative wearable sculpture.

Esmée Peets works across painting, graphite drawing, and collaborative wearable sculpture — close-up subjects (hands, necks, the side of a face) rendered in colour that doesn't hold back.

She is currently studying Art History and Studio Arts at Concordia University in Montréal, following a Visual Arts concentration at École secondaire publique De La Salle in Ottawa. Her studio practice grew out of that program, culminating in a cohesive series of large-format acrylic paintings in deep violet and magenta.

Alongside her own work, Esmée has spent time on the other side of the gallery wall: as a Community Programs Assistant in the Learning & Engagement department at the Ottawa Art Gallery, she's programmed summer art camps, led gallery tours, coordinated guest-artist workshops, and run accessible art workshops for adults with disabilities. She's also mounted large-scale exhibitions and built workshop material kits as a Visual Arts Assistant at the Centre d'Excellence Artistique de l'Ontario.

She has taught private drawing lessons since 2022 and has led group art instruction for children, developing individualized lesson plans and adapting her teaching to different ages and skill levels. She is bilingual (English/French) and has sung soprano in Ottawa choirs since 2015.
